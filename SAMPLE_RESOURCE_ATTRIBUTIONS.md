# AI Composer — Sample Resource Credits

The composer retains its own code and arrangements. Recorded sample libraries belong to their respective creators and are not generated by the AI Composer.

## Greg Sullivan Wurlitzer EP200

- **Instrument:** Wurlitzer EP200 electric piano, recorded by Greg Sullivan; original mapping converted to SFZ by kinwie.
- **Source:** https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- **Adaptation:** The build produces a separate `composer-wurlitzer.sfz` with source-defined preprocessor constants expanded for sfizz compatibility. It does not change or substitute the original recorded FLAC samples.
- **Testing requirement:** The composer must verify all SFZ sample references and render non-silent audio before accepting this instrument.

The other approved Karoryfer resources retain their existing upstream source, CC0 license, and resource registry entries. No output should silently substitute GM proof voices or synthesized timbres for sampled acoustic or electromechanical instruments.

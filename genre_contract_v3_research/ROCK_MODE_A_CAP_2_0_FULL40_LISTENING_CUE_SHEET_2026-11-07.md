# Rock Mode A 2.0 vs 2.8 — full-40 listening cue sheet

Date: 2026-11-07  
Branch: `genre-contract-v3-no-reasoning-diagnostic`  
Status: diagnostic only; no canonical policy change.

## What differs

Both full-length context mixes use:
- the same 40-bar Rock symbolic composition,
- the same treated LEAD performance,
- the same Shinyguitar resource and mapping,
- HARMONY at -5 dB,
- LEAD at -6 dB,
- the same tempo and form.

The only difference is this:

- **2.0 candidate:** positive HARMONY extensions are capped at 2.0 beats.
- **2.8 control:** current canonical Mode A allows those extreme HARMONY gestures to ring to 2.8 beats.

Exactly 26 vertical rhythm-guitar gestures differ, distributed across every major section.

## Listening rule

Do **not** choose the candidate merely because it sounds shorter or cleaner.

Prefer the shortest cap that:
- stops the guitar from sounding chopped,
- preserves natural chord ring,
- leaves intentional air between gestures,
- does not turn the HARMONY part into a pad,
- does not weaken section momentum,
- does not make choruses feel smaller than verses.

If 2.0 sounds equally natural or more natural than 2.8, the extra 0.8 beat has no demonstrated musical value and should not remain by default.

If 2.0 sounds noticeably too clipped in multiple sections, keep 2.8 for now and revisit the duration rule rather than forcing the cap.

## Decision boundary

A **2.0 approval** requires that it sound natural across intro, both verses, both choruses, and outro—not merely in one favorable passage.

A **2.8 retention** requires audible evidence that the extra sustain materially improves the music somewhere important without causing smear elsewhere.

If neither version sounds right, do not split the difference automatically. That would indicate that the duration policy itself—not just the maximum cap—needs another look.

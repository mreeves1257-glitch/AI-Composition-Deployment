# Rock Mode A HARMONY 2.0 cap — decision gate

Date: 2026-11-07  
Branch: `genre-contract-v3-no-reasoning-diagnostic`

## Current evidence

The 2.0-beat HARMONY cap is structurally cleaner than the current 2.8-beat ceiling in the narrow sense that it removes only the extreme long-ring class while preserving all moderate-duration classes.

That does **not** constitute musical approval.

## What has been proven

- Current 2.8-beat extreme HARMONY sustains are governed by the hard `max_ringing_beats` ceiling.
- The 2.0 diagnostic changes only that ceiling.
- 78 HARMONY note-events / 26 vertical chord gestures are affected over the 40-bar form.
- The affected gestures occur in every major section.
- Each affected gesture loses exactly 0.8 beat of symbolic ring.
- Pitch, onset, velocity, voicing, event count and instrument identity remain unchanged.
- Canonical HARMONY shortenings remain unchanged.
- LEAD performance remains unchanged.
- Bass and drums remain unchanged.
- No reasoning-engine behavior is involved.
- No live or canonical policy change has been made.

## What is not yet proven

- That 2.0 sounds better over the full 40-bar form.
- That 2.0 preserves adequate chorus weight.
- That 2.0 preserves a convincing outro.
- That 2.0 is preferable to 2.8 after repeated exposure across both verses and choruses.
- That the current duration model is otherwise complete.

## Approval rule

Do not change the canonical `max_ringing_beats` from 2.8 to 2.0 unless the full-40 human comparison shows that 2.0:

1. remains natural through intro, verses, choruses and outro;
2. does not sound chopped;
3. preserves guitar-like chord decay;
4. preserves chorus lift and ending weight;
5. reduces smear or unnecessary continuous ringing compared with 2.8.

If 2.0 and 2.8 are perceptually equivalent, prefer 2.0 because it is the narrower, less invasive sustain policy.

If 2.8 is clearly better in important sections, retain 2.8.

If both sound wrong, do not choose an intermediate cap automatically. Reopen the duration policy itself.

## Promotion boundary

Even a successful full-40 listening approval promotes 2.0 only to the diagnostic branch's preferred Mode A HARMONY cap candidate. It does not automatically merge to main or activate the live Composer.

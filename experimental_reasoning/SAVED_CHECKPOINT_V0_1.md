# Composer Reasoning Engine v0.1 — Saved Checkpoint

Saved as an intentional development checkpoint on November 6, 2026 (America/Chicago).

## Status

This module is experimental and isolated from the live Composer. It is preserved as a reusable reasoning-engine prototype and must not be discarded merely because later versions supersede it.

## Preserved capabilities

- equal compositional importance across active instruments;
- whole-composition instrument importance ledger;
- instrument interaction and role handoff;
- abstract musical-idea memory;
- transformed recall, dormancy, and cross-instrument idea transfer;
- maximum rich/deep bass character target;
- pronounced physical kick target;
- kick/bass role separation and musical interaction;
- early 3D spatial intent with late existing-mixer realization;
- section-aware spatial choreography;
- protection against random spatial gimmicks;
- genre/interpreter authority preserved;
- no MIDI, note-event, sample, or audio-render authority in v0.1;
- decision hashing and isolation guardrails.

## Core low-end target

Kick = physical impact and pulse definition.

Bass = sustained depth, warmth, tonal body, and movement.

Both remain compositionally equal with the other active instruments. Richness is achieved through role, timing, register, interaction, space, and later 3D realization rather than simple loudness.

## Architecture rule

Plan spatial relationships during reasoning, but preserve the existing downstream 3D mixer as the physical audio realization stage.

## Preservation rule

Keep this checkpoint available even after v0.2 or later work begins. Future changes should continue on the working branch or a new development branch rather than rewriting the saved snapshot.

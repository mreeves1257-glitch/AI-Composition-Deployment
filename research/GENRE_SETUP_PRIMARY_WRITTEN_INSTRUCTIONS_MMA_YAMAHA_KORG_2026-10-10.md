## VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENTATION

1. MMA (Musical MIDI Accompaniment), Bob van der Poel, **main reference and installing/running**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/ref/mma.html and https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/ref/node1.html . The program consumes tempo, meter, chords and text-format track patterns, sequences and Groove definitions to generate MIDI. This is composition/instruction, NOT actual sampled instrument audio.
2. **MMA original pattern grammar**: https://mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/ref/node4.html . Distinguishes rhythmic pattern classes for Walk (walking bass), Bass, Chord, Drum, Arpeggio, etc.
3. **MMA sequences**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/ref/node5.html . Use track-specific multi-bar sequences, distinct rhythmic patterns per role.
4. **MMA grooves**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/ref/node6.html . Define, select and rotate complete named groove variations via `DefGroove` and `Groove`.
5. **MMA Swing timing**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/ref/node18.html . Critically, `SwingMode On` affects pattern COMPILATION; merely setting SwingMode AFTER a groove is compiled is ineffective. This timing must be defined with the style's source or intentionally handled at MIDI performance handoff. Do not confuse with renaming a straight groove to Swing.
6. **MMA standard library**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/lib/index.html . Contains verified style categories such as stdlib/swing, easyswing, fastswing, jazzcombo, bebop, bigband, jazzwaltz, salsa, westernswing, blues, jazz fusion and much more; separate family/style musical grammar is ALREADY WRITTEN.
7. **MMA actual Swing style reference**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/lib/stdlib/swing.html . Swing, SwingWalk, SwingPlus, SwingTriple, SwingIntro, SwingFill, SwingEnd, alternate arrangements; written roles and multi-bar patterns. Upstream source `infojunkie/mma` `lib/stdlib/swing.mma`, Git source blob SHA `5902928a9a13ffcd8ca660d7dd2a58033982a14f`.
8. **MMA Yamaha-derived Jazz Swing**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/lib/yamaha/jazzswing.html . Recorded in MIDI pattern format with `JazzSwingMainA` 12-bar, MainB 12-bar, one-bar FillAA/FillBB, two-bar IntroA and EndingA; independent walking bass, piano chord and drum tracks. Upstream source `infojunkie/mma` `lib/yamaha/jazzswing.mma`, Git blob SHA `5944b4cad8b37719bf912b58414475c9a3189258`. Does NOT supply a sampled solo trumpet: preserve Swing's independent original trumpet melody source instead.
9. **MMA Jazz Combo additional reference**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/lib/stdlib/jazzcombo.html ; `lib/stdlib/jazzcombo.mma` blob SHA `64e24b2ff96a93656719ae1b4b8754018bd0825a`. Distinct chord-guitar, piano, electric piano patterns, walking bass and multiple kit instruments.
10. **Korg manufacturer arranger setup**: https://www.manualzz.com/doc/o/2pujex/korg-pa5x-user-manual-recording-and-editing-the-styles-and-pads ; see also official Pa300 PDF https://cdn.korg.com/us/support/download/files/3bc0ef8f49b76baae33ab2aa1818f208.pdf . Korg explicitly separates Intro/Variation/Fill/Ending **Style Elements** and **Chord Variations**, one pattern per track with track-specific chord transposition; Drum/Perc do NOT chord-transpose; Bass/Acc use NTT; optional Guitar tracks receive special interpretation. Critically Korg separates RECORDING THE STYLE PATTERNS from EDITING THE TRACK SOUND SETTINGS (volume, pan, FX), affirming user's instruction to keep sound settings unchanged now.
11. **MMA comprehensive tutorial**: https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/tut/mma-tutorial.html and https://www.mellowood.ca/mma/online-docs/html/tut/node5.html — actual implementation in four stages: define track patterns, assemble sequences, save/activate named grooves, compose a song from chord changes, render MIDI via MMA. More than 1000 grooves in distribution; do not write 55 entirely new genre engines from scratch.

## Setup prescription to follow for EVERY genre (no new 55-off-the-cuff styles)

1. Select the existing original genre profile. Pick an authentic external **written groove/style source** where available; never assume two similarly named styles sound the same.
2. Identify its main, variations, intro, fills, ending, track roles, chord mappings, swing/straight timing and section lengths. Preserve the actual rhythm logic using the source's grammar rather than a label or generic metronome.
3. Run one genuine music-generator/interpreter under that genre's slot. Feed the **actual Composer MIDI/chords** and verify separate identity and correct order; avoid running two competing music composers in series without a defined contract.
4. Verify **each resulting MIDI role** maps to exactly the original user-requested recorded SFZ instrument identity and trigger note zones; unsupported notes/recordings BLOCK audio approval.
5. Render original SFZ instrument stems, sum cleanly to standard stereo, listen for clear distinct natural instruments and correct genre rhythm. Leave mix, brush volume, clarinet sustain, piano settings and 3D unchanged for this round.
6. Only after actual song audio and listener approval mark its genre playable; labels, unit-test notes or simple 7-bar source seeds do not constitute full activation.
